# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** July 3, 2026
**Last updated:** July 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Tailor Trends, LLC** ("we," "us," or "our"), the operator of **TailorMade Coloring Book** (the "Service," available at tailormadecoloringbook.app), collects, uses, shares, and protects information.

## 1. A note about children — please read this first

TailorMade Coloring Book creates personalized coloring books **for** children, but it is designed to be purchased and operated **by** a parent or legal guardian who is at least 18 years old. Children do not create accounts, do not log in, and do not interact directly with the Service. A parent or guardian sets up the account, provides limited information about their child (a first name and an age range) to personalize the content, and receives the finished coloring book as a PDF.

Because the Service is intended for children's content, we treat information a parent provides about a child as protected under the **Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)** and handle it with the heightened care described in Section 8 below.

## 2. Who this policy applies to

This policy applies to parents and guardians who create an account and use the Service, and to the limited information they provide about the children for whom books are made. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the Service.

## 3. Information we collect

**Information you provide directly:**

- **Account information.** When you sign in (via Google Sign-In through Firebase Authentication), we receive your name and email address associated with that account.
- **Child profile information.** To personalize a coloring book, you may create one or more child profiles containing the child's **first name** and an **age range**. We do not require or request a child's last name, date of birth, home address, photograph, or contact information.
- **Book inputs.** The story, theme, or description you enter to generate a book.
- **Payment information.** When you subscribe, payment is processed by **Stripe**. We do not collect or store your full card number; Stripe handles that directly. We receive limited billing metadata (such as subscription status, the last four digits of the card, and transaction identifiers).
- **Communications.** If you email or contact us, we keep that correspondence.

**Information collected automatically:**

- **Usage and technical data.** Basic usage information (such as books generated and subscription usage against your plan limits), and technical data such as IP address, browser type, and device information collected through our hosting and error-monitoring providers for security, debugging, and abuse prevention.

We do **not** use behavioral or targeted advertising, and we do **not** track children across other websites or services.

## 4. How we use information

We use the information described above to:

- Create, generate, and deliver personalized coloring books;
- Run content-safety checks on inputs and generated scenes;
- Set up and maintain your account and authenticate you;
- Process your subscription, payments, and renewals;
- Send transactional email (for example, receipts, delivery notifications, and account or security messages);
- Enforce plan limits and prevent fraud, abuse, and misuse;
- Diagnose and fix technical problems and improve reliability; and
- Comply with legal obligations.

We do **not** sell personal information, and we do **not** use children's personal information for behavioral advertising or to build profiles for purposes unrelated to providing the Service.

## 5. How the AI generation works, and what data it processes

To generate a book, the story or theme you provide — and, where relevant, the child's first name and age range used to personalize content — is sent to our AI service providers:

- **Anthropic (Claude)** performs scene planning and content-safety screening.
- **fal.ai** generates the line-art images.

These providers act as **service providers processing data on our behalf** to deliver the Service you requested. Under our agreements and their terms, this data is used only to provide the generation service and is **not used to train their AI models** or for their own independent purposes. Generated images and finished PDFs are stored on **Cloudflare R2** so you can access and download your books.

## 6. How we share information

We share information only in these circumstances:

- **With service providers ("sub-processors")** who help us operate the Service, each acting on our instructions:

  | Provider | Purpose |
  |---|---|
  | Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore) | Sign-in and database |
  | Cloudflare R2 | Storage of generated images and PDFs |
  | Stripe | Payment processing |
  | Anthropic (Claude) | Scene planning and content safety |
  | fal.ai | Image generation |
  | Resend | Transactional email |
  | Sentry | Error monitoring and debugging |
  | Vercel / Railway | Application hosting |

- **For legal reasons**, when we believe in good faith that disclosure is required to comply with law, respond to lawful requests, or protect the rights, safety, or property of our users or the public.
- **In a business transfer**, such as a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to this policy's protections.

We do **not** disclose a child's personal information to third parties for targeted advertising or any purpose that is not integral to providing the Service.

## 7. Data retention

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, and we do not retain children's personal information indefinitely. Our retention schedule:

| Data | Retention |
|---|---|
| Child profile information (first name, age range) | Deleted immediately when you remove the profile in the app. |
| Book inputs and generated books/PDFs | Deleted immediately when you remove that book in the app. |
| Account information | Deleted, along with all associated child profiles, books, and generated files, when you request account deletion. See Section 10. |
| Payment/billing metadata | Retained as required for tax, accounting, and fraud-prevention purposes (transaction records held primarily by Stripe). |
| Technical logs / error data | Retained per our infrastructure providers' standard retention windows, then purged. |

We do not retain children's personal information indefinitely, and we do not use it for any purpose beyond providing the Service.

## 8. Children's privacy (COPPA)

We are committed to protecting children's privacy and comply with COPPA.

**What we collect about children.** Only what a parent voluntarily provides to personalize a book: the child's **first name** and **age range**, plus the story or theme the parent enters. We do not knowingly collect any other personal information from or about children, and children cannot submit information to us directly.

**Parental consent.** A parent or guardian provides this information and, by creating a child profile and generating books, consents to our use of it solely to provide the Service. We do not disclose children's personal information to third parties except to the service providers listed in Section 6 that are integral to delivering the Service.

**No advertising or profiling.** We do not use children's personal information for behavioral advertising, and we do not sell it.

**Parental rights.** As a parent or guardian, you may at any time:

- **Review** the personal information we have collected about your child;
- **Delete** your child's profile and associated data;
- **Refuse** to permit further collection or use of your child's information (by deleting the profile or closing the account).

To exercise these rights, use the in-app account controls or contact us at **privacy@tailormadecoloringbook.com**. We will verify that the request comes from the parent or guardian on the account before acting on it.

**Security program.** We maintain a written information-security program with safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the information we handle, including encrypted transport, access controls, and reputable infrastructure providers.

## 9. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of your personal information, or to object to certain processing (for example, under the California Consumer Privacy Act or the EU/UK GDPR). We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. To exercise any right, contact us at **privacy@tailormadecoloringbook.com**; we will respond as required by applicable law and will not discriminate against you for exercising a right.

## 10. Account and data deletion

You can delete individual child profiles and books at any time from within the app, and this takes effect immediately. Full account deletion — removing your account along with all associated child profiles, books, and generated files in one action — is available from your Profile page (Delete Account). You may also request full deletion by emailing **privacy@tailormadecoloringbook.com**, and we will complete a verified request within 30 days.

## 11. Data security

We use industry-standard measures — encryption in transit, authenticated access, reputable managed infrastructure, and error monitoring — to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 12. International users

We operate the Service from the United States, and your information will be processed there. If you access the Service from outside the United States, you consent to processing in the U.S., which may have different data-protection laws than your jurisdiction.

## 13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make a material change to how we handle children's personal information, we will provide notice and, where required, obtain renewed parental consent. The "Last updated" date reflects the most recent version.
